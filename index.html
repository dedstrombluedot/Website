About

Perspective Changes Everything

In 1999, I was asked to create a model to predict employee retention risk. I fit a logistic regression model using SAS. After collaborating with HR to determine the necessary data inputs, as well as the evaluation and implementation of my model, I was hooked. Since then, I have built and scaled data infrastructure and data teams with the goal of helping businesses make critical decisions using data.

I enjoy working across any business vertical, and focus on people, processes, and business needs. Data is only valuable if it is curated, documented and trusted by its end users. Data must adhere to availability constraints, and must come with a plan to make it usable to the organization and, ultimately, ready for the demands of robust AI models. I have spent my career building and scaling this type of infrastructure and people-empowered backbone. Data is a critical business asset that requires a dedicated and thoughtful approach.

On a personal level, I derive guidance from growing up on a small dairy farm with my parents and five siblings. This background taught me a belief in hard work, dedication to a common goal, strong collaboration and communication, and the power of community. I’d love to hear your story, and how I may be able to help your organization use data to its full potential.
